Which files

You can upload PDF, Word and image files up to 50 MB each.

Accepted types

PDF, DOCX, JPG and PNG. Other types are refused before the upload starts.

Size

Larger files can be split, or compressed before uploading.

After the upload

The document appears in the archive with the title you gave it.

Renaming

Open the document and change its title: the file itself does not change.
